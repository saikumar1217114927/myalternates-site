XIRR calculator.

When money goes in and comes out on irregular dates — top-ups, partial withdrawals, SIP instalments — a single "CAGR" no longer describes the return. XIRR is the annualised rate that accounts for the exact timing of every cash flow. For each line, pick Purchase, Redemption or Current value and enter the amount — we handle the plus or minus.

Purchase = money you invested. Redemption = money you took out. Current value = what the holding is worth today — use this on the last row, dated today, if you haven't fully exited.
