The basics

What are GIFT City products?

GIFT City, in Gujarat, houses India's International Financial Services Centre (IFSC) — a dedicated financial zone with its own regulator, the IFSCA. Funds set up there are run by IFSCA-registered Fund Management Entities and transact in foreign currency, usually US dollars, giving Indian investors a regulated onshore route to international markets.

Resident individuals invest through the RBI's Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS), which allows remittance of up to USD 250,000 per financial year for permitted purposes, including investment in IFSC funds. Depending on the structure, you can access global equity and debt strategies, feeder funds into offshore managers, and other international mandates.

IFSC units and certain investor income carry tax concessions, but the treatment depends on the fund, your residency and the year of investment — this is an area to work through with a tax adviser before you commit.

Why it matters

Why invest through GIFT City

For an investor looking to diversify outside India, GIFT City is often the cleanest way to do it:

  • Global market and currency exposure that isn't tied to the rupee or Indian equities
  • A regulated Indian jurisdiction — IFSCA oversight, Indian courts, familiar reporting
  • A structured, transparent use of your LRS limit rather than piecemeal overseas transfers
  • Access to international strategies and managers packaged into IFSC funds
  • Tax concessions available to IFSC structures, subject to your own position

Eligibility, the remittance mechanics and the currency and tax implications all need to be right before you invest — that's the part we help you work through.
