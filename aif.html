The basics

What is an AIF?

REPLACE THIS BLOCK with your own explanation — the SEBI AIF framework, Categories I / II / III and examples of each, ₹1 crore minimum commitment, drawdown vs upfront, lock-in and liquidity.

An Alternative Investment Fund (AIF) is a privately pooled, SEBI-registered vehicle that invests in assets and strategies outside traditional stocks and bonds — private equity, venture capital, private credit, long-short and more.

AIFs are grouped into Category I (e.g. venture, infrastructure), Category II (e.g. private equity, private credit) and Category III (e.g. hedge-fund-style long-short), each with a different risk and return profile.
