The basics

What is PMS?

Portfolio Management Services (PMS) is a professionally managed investment service in which a SEBI-registered portfolio manager builds and runs a portfolio of stocks — and sometimes other securities — on your behalf, following a stated strategy and your risk profile.

The key difference from a mutual fund is ownership. In PMS the securities sit in your own demat account rather than in a pooled scheme, portfolios are more concentrated (typically 15–30 stocks instead of 50-plus), and you can see every holding, every trade and every charge.

PMS is regulated by SEBI with a minimum investment of ₹50 lakh, so it is aimed at high-net-worth investors who want a portfolio shaped around their goals, tax position and views, with direct access to the manager's thinking. Most mandates are discretionary — the manager decides and executes within the agreed strategy; non-discretionary and advisory mandates, where you sign off on each decision, are also available.

Why it matters

Why invest in PMS

For an investor with the ticket size to qualify, PMS offers a degree of control and visibility that pooled products cannot:

  • Direct ownership of every security, held in your own demat account
  • Concentrated, high-conviction portfolios with a clear, stated strategy
  • Full transparency on holdings, transactions, costs and performance
  • Access to specialised managers and styles not offered as mutual funds
  • Portfolios that can be tailored to your risk appetite, horizon and tax position
  • A single relationship for reviews, rebalancing and reporting

The trade-offs are a higher entry point, fees that vary widely between managers, and returns that depend heavily on the manager you choose — which is where the fee comparison and our research desk come in.
