The basics

What is a Specialised Investment Fund?

The Specialised Investment Fund (SIF) is a category introduced by SEBI in 2025 to fill the gap between mutual funds and the far larger tickets of PMS and AIF. It keeps a pooled, regulated structure but allows managers to run more flexible, higher-conviction strategies — including long-short equity and other approaches a traditional mutual fund is not permitted to use.

SIFs are offered by established mutual fund houses under a distinct brand, with a minimum investment of ₹10 lakh per investor across an AMC's SIF strategies (accredited investors aside). That places sophisticated strategies within reach of investors who don't have ₹50 lakh for PMS or ₹1 crore for an AIF.

Because the framework is new, the range of live strategies is still growing. We track what launches, screen it, and surface the ones worth considering rather than every product that comes to market.

Why it matters

Why invest in SIF

SIF is worth understanding if you want more than a plain long-only fund but aren't ready to commit PMS or AIF-sized capital:

  • Access to strategies — like long-short equity — previously reserved for large tickets
  • A regulated, pooled structure with SEBI-defined investment and risk limits
  • Return streams that can behave differently from a standard equity or debt fund
  • Run by the research and risk teams of established mutual fund houses
  • A ₹10 lakh entry point rather than ₹50 lakh or ₹1 crore

As with any newer, more flexible strategy, outcomes vary by manager and approach, and some SIFs carry higher risk than a traditional fund — read the scheme documents and talk to us before committing.
