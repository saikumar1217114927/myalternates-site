About us

India's platform for alternate investments.

myAlternates is built and run by people who have spent their careers inside India's capital markets — and built for investors who want every alternate they hold held to the same standard.

myAlternates is India's platform for alternate investments, powered by PMS Bazaar — focused exclusively on the strategies that, over the long term, drive better risk-adjusted returns.

We give a 21st-century digital twist to traditional wealth advisory, investment advisory and equity-market specialisation for high-net-worth individuals and families who want to stay ahead of the curve.

At the core of our DNA is close alignment with investor outcomes — which is what leads to transparent pricing, long-term relationships and service standards personalised to the ever-changing needs of individuals and families.

Our journey

From a two-person idea in 2017 to ₹2,200 Cr on one dashboard.

Eight years of onboarding investors, renegotiating fees with AMCs and building both dashboards — here's how it added up.

2017
Day one

Two founders, one conviction

Hameed Rahman and D. Rajesh Kumar lay the foundation stone for myAlternates under PMS Bazaar — seamless onboarding for investors, and a single live view for every alternate they hold.

2018
₹0Cr ~140 investors onboarded

The first fee we ever renegotiated

The first AMC pass-through deal is signed. Investors start keeping more of every rupee of return instead of losing it to mark-ups.

2019
₹0Cr 520 investors · distributor dashboard live

Both sides of the table, one engine

The distributor dashboard goes live and 25+ AMCs move to platform-negotiated pricing — passed straight through to investors, no hidden spread.

2020
₹0Cr 850+ investors · 98% stayed through the COVID drawdown

Investors kept the faith

Through the sharpest drawdown in a decade, almost no one exited — and inflows kept coming. GIFT City access was added so investors could diversify offshore from the same login.

2021
₹0Cr 1,200 investors

A research desk of our own

Unbiased research is formalised and quarterly manager-access calls begin — investors speak directly to the people running the money.

2022
₹0Cr 320+ distributor logins

The lowest-PMS-fee commitment

Every PMS on the platform is renegotiated with its AMC. The saving stops being a pitch and becomes a promise passed through to every investor.

2023
₹0Cr 110+ AMC integrations

SIF and AIF, made simple

Onboarding for the newest categories is streamlined end-to-end, so specialised and private-market strategies sit in the same view as everything else.

2025
₹0Cr 2,500+ investors · 600+ distributors

Where we are now

150+ partner AMCs. Five products. Two dashboards. One data engine — and the same alignment with investor outcomes we started with.
