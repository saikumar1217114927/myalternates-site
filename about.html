About us

India's platform for alternate investments.

myAlternates is built and run by people who have spent their careers inside India's capital markets — and built for investors who want every alternate they hold held to the same standard.

myAlternates is India's platform for alternate investments, powered by PMS Bazaar — focused exclusively on the strategies that, over the long term, drive better risk-adjusted returns.

We give a 21st-century digital twist to traditional wealth advisory, investment advisory and equity-market specialisation for high-net-worth individuals and families who want to stay ahead of the curve.

At the core of our DNA is close alignment with investor outcomes — which is what leads to transparent pricing, long-term relationships and service standards personalised to the ever-changing needs of individuals and families.

Our management team

Hameed Rahman

Founder & Director

Hameed Rahman brings 16 years of marketing experience across Capital Markets, Banking, Insurance and Real Estate. He has held senior roles at several reputed financial institutions and previously served as Regional Head of South India for Motilal Oswal. His investor-centric approach and market acumen have shaped the marketing strategy that gave myAlternates its decisive direction as a brand.

D. Rajesh Kumar

Founder & Director

D. Rajesh Kumar carries 11 years of experience in financial markets, built on a core belief in investor-centric client relationships. With a solid background in banking, Rajesh moved into the financial markets and found his calling there. He was a pivotal part of the sales team at Motilal Oswal before joining Hameed to start myAlternates.

K. S. Ramachandran

Director

K. S. Ramachandran has been drawn to stock-broking and wealth management from an early age, despite training as a mechanical engineer. He became a Madras Stock Exchange sub-broker in 1991 and, in 2001, a Motilal Oswal franchise owner — going on to run the No. 1 franchise in South India, with over 12,000 clients by 2005. With more than 20 years in wealth management, he brings a deep understanding of the financial sector and a focus on personalised investment solutions for clients.
