CAGR calculator.

CAGR — compound annual growth rate — is the single yearly rate that would take your starting amount to its ending value over the holding period, as if it grew by exactly that percentage every year. Because it compounds, it's almost always lower than a simple "total return ÷ years" figure, and it's the fairer way to compare investments held for different lengths of time.
